# 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Weight-Loss Meal Plan

**Target Demographic:** 40-Year-Old Working Professional  
**Activity Level:** Sedentary / Long-sitting desk job  
**Dietary Focus:** South Indian Vegetarian, High Fiber, Calorie & Portion-Controlled, Balanced Macros  

---

### Nutritional Strategy & Dietician Notes:
1. **Calorie & Macro Distribution:** Designed to maintain a moderate caloric deficit (approx. 1400–1500 kcal/day) without extreme restriction.
2. **Protein Focus:** Higher proportion of lentils, pulses, paneer, and sprouts to compensate for a desk-bound lifestyle and keep satiety high.
3. **Carbohydrate Swaps:** Fiber-rich grains (millet, brown rice, oats, red rice) replace refined white rice and white flour.
4. **Hydration & Gut Health:** Buttermilk (Mor/Majjiga) is featured daily to aid digestion during long working hours.

### Daily Guidelines for Best Weight-Loss Results:
* **Cooking Oil:** Use maximum 2-3 teaspoons per day total (Cold-pressed Gingelly/Sesame oil, Mustard oil, or Groundnut oil preferred).
* **Hydration:** Consume 2.5 to 3 liters of water daily. Keep a water bottle at your desk.
* **Desk Mobility:** Incorporate a 5-minute walking break every 60 minutes during office hours.
