# 7-Day Healthy South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan for Weight Loss

**Target Audience:** 60-year-old active male  
**Dietary Focus:** South Indian Vegetarian, Weight Loss, Gut-Friendly (Mild Spices for Ulcers), Peanut-Free, Coffee-Free  
**Nutritional Highlights:** High Fiber, Lean Plant Protein, Complex Carbohydrates, Low Glycemic Index

---

### Dietary Guidelines & Tips
* **Spice Adjustments:** Keep green chillies, red chilli powder, and black pepper to a minimum to avoid triggering stomach ulcer irritation. Focus on mild aromatics like cumin (*jeera*), mustard seeds, curry leaves, ginger, and coriander seeds.
* **Hydration:** Drink at least 2.5–3 liters of water daily. Warm water or mild cumin water (*jeera water*) works well after meals.
* **Cooking Oil:** Limit cooking oil to 1–2 teaspoons per day per person (preferably sesame/til oil or ghee in minimal quantities).
* **Peanut Avoidance:** Replaced traditional peanut chutney/chikki with coconut-coriander chutney, roasted chana dal chutney, sesame seeds, or roasted seeds.

### Important Lifestyle & Ulcer-Care Notes
1. **Meal Timing:** Eat dinner at least 2 to 2.5 hours before bedtime to avoid night-time reflux or ulcer pain.
2. **Probiotics:** Homemade plain curd and buttermilk (*mor*) infused with roasted cumin seeds and coriander will aid gut healing and digestion.
3. **Hydration for Weight Loss:** Drink water 30 minutes before meals rather than during or immediately after meals.
